FOR THE FIRST RIDE

First Tracks

Make the lake feel a little more familiar. This introductory session focuses on equipment orientation, communicating with the boat crew, and understanding a balanced starting position.

  • Understand the session and hand signals before entering the water.
  • Practice a relaxed setup and a controlled deep-water start.
  • Build toward a short, comfortable ride on two skis.

Session focus: A confident first start, one attempt at a time.

FOR THE NEXT BREAKTHROUGH

Find Your Edge

Already getting up? Turn a successful start into a more consistent ride. Explore posture, handle position, and controlled changes of direction, with feedback matched to your current ability.

  • Identify one habit that affects your balance.
  • Develop smoother, more deliberate edge changes.
  • Explore a progression toward single-ski riding when appropriate.

Session focus: More control, less wasted effort.

FOR THE NEXT BUOY

Course Ready

For skiers comfortable on one ski, the next challenge is repeatability. Work on rhythm and approach with a coach, using realistic goals rather than rushing to a faster speed or shorter rope.

  • Review your current slalom experience and goals.
  • Choose a focus such as timing or turn consistency.
  • Reflect after each set and set a practical next milestone.

Session focus: Consistency before intensity.

HOW A SESSION COMES TOGETHER

Simple on purpose.

Talk it through

Share what you’ve tried, what feels challenging, and what you’d like to achieve. Agree on an appropriate plan with your coach.

Take a focused set

After a briefing and equipment check, work on one manageable skill with an attentive driver and spotter.

Leave with a next step

Review what changed and what still needs practice. Pick one focus to carry into your next session.

BEFORE YOU GET ON THE WATER

A few good
questions.

Do I need my own equipment?

For this business concept, equipment needs would be discussed before booking. Suitability, sizing, and availability would be confirmed with the operator.

What should I bring?

Swimwear, a towel, drinking water, and sun protection are useful starting points. Confirm equipment, flotation requirements, and site rules with your instructor before attending.

Can I book a session here?

SkiReady is a class project, so this website does not accept bookings or payments. The programs demonstrate how a coaching business could help visitors choose a training direction.

How do I choose a program?

Try the training matcher on the home page. It gives an illustrative starting point; an instructor would confirm your readiness and adapt the session.
